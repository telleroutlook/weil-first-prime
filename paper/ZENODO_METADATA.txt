## Description (paste as-is into the Abstract field)
We establish six structural results for the local Weil quadratic form
Q_W^L on L^2(-L,L) in the first-prime window (½log2 < L < ½log3),
the minimal interval where only the prime n=2 contributes to the
Weil explicit formula.

Our main contribution (Theorem 3) is a pure-rational absorption
certificate showing that at L = 7/20 the log endpoint potential V
absorbs the first-prime layer: V + P_{2,7/20} ≥ (69/100)V ≥ 0.
The certificate reduces to five integer comparisons, including the key
step 87^16 * 68^5 < 1701^5 * 32^16 (39-digit integers), independently
machine-verified by Lean 4's native_decide tactic.

We also give an exact spectral description of the truncated shift
operator C_{b,L} (Theorem 1), prove that Path A (potential
redistribution) is strictly falsified by two explicit negative
witnesses (Theorem 6), and provide the algebraic and analytic
framework for the Path B Schur criterion (Theorems 4–5).

All results are supported by a certificate-first proof infrastructure:
fail-closed JSON schemas, dual-path interval arithmetic (Path A ∩
Path B verified for all basis entries), and 123 automated tests.
The supporting code and Lean 4 formalisation are available at
https://github.com/telleroutlook/weil-first-prime

These results are independent of the main conjecture FP-0.35
(λ(7/20) > 0) and do not imply the Riemann Hypothesis.
