Title:
  "Path A is Structurally Obstructed Throughout the First-Prime Window"

Summary:
  The main theorem proves that the "Path A" sufficient condition for FP-0.35
  — the weakened form q~_L = T + theta*V + K_L - c_L*I being positive — fails
  for every L in the first-prime window (½log2, ½log3). The obstruction has
  two explicit regimes determined by the critical point L* = (2/3)log2 ≈ 0.462:
  (i) For L < L*: Path A fails when theta < 1 - c_2/kappa_edge(L).
  (ii) For L >= L*: Path A fails for ALL theta < 1, unconditionally.
  This result is independent of the main conjecture and does not imply RH.
