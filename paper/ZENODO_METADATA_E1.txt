## Description
We prove that the "Path A" sufficient condition for the FP-0.35 conjecture
— that the weakened form q~_L = T + theta*V + K_L - c_L*I is strictly
positive — fails for every L in the first-prime window (half*log2, half*log3).
The obstruction has two regimes. For L in (half*log2, L*) where L* = (2/3)*log2
approximately 0.462, Path A fails whenever the redistribution coefficient
theta < 1 - c_2/kappa_edge(L); the threshold is explicit and strictly positive.
For L in [L*, half*log3), the edge-absorption inequality reverses
(kappa_edge(L) <= 0), and Path A fails for ALL theta < 1 regardless of the
redistribution coefficient. These results are independent of the main conjecture
FP-0.35 and do not imply the Riemann Hypothesis. The companion paper
(doi:10.5281/zenodo.21807498) establishes that Path B (the split-residual Schur
criterion) is not subject to this obstruction.
