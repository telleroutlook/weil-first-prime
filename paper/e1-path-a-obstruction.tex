\begin{abstract}
We give a self-contained account of why the Path A sufficient condition
$\tilde{q}_L^\theta := T + \theta V + K_L - c_L I \ge 0$ fails at $L = 7/20$
and $\theta = 69/100$.
An independent 128-bit Arb computation clarifies the spectral mechanism:
the negative direction is driven by the \emph{Weil constant}
$c_L \approx 1.365$ acting as a global negative shift, not by a
competition between the Archimedean kernel and edge-mass absorption.
As a byproduct, we identify a failure of the asymptotic
$\mu_K^{(02)} \sim \ctwo/\kappae(L)$ (companion Lemma~L3) in the interior
of the first-prime window, which invalidates the previously proposed
formula $\theta_0(L) = 1 - \ctwo/\kappae(L)$ for the off-diagonal
flip point.
All numerical claims are verified by the scripts in
\texttt{src/verify\_2x2\_gram.py} and \texttt{checker/thm6/check\_thm6.py}.
\end{abstract}

\section{Setup and notation}

Let $L = 7/20$, $\theta = 69/100$, $c_L \approx 1.36527$ (Arb-certified to
256-bit precision from the Bombieri--Weil explicit formula;
see~\cite{WFP2026} Remark~2.1).
The Weil quadratic form is
\[
  Q_W^L(v)
  = \ip{Tv}{v} + \ip{Vv}{v} + \ip{K_Lv}{v}
    - \ctwo\ip{C_{\log2,L}v}{v},
\]
where $V(x) = -\tfrac12\log(1-x^2)$, $K_L$ has kernel
$k_L(x,y) = -L\,r''(L(x-y))$, $C_{\log2,L}$ is the prime-$2$
convolution operator, and $\ctwo = \log2/\sqrt2$.

The Path~A sufficient condition for $Q_W^L \ge 0$ replaces the prime term
by absorbing it into $\theta V$:
\[
  \tilde{q}_L^\theta := T + \theta V + K_L - c_L I.
\]
Theorem~6 of~\cite{WFP2026} shows this condition is false at
$(L,\theta) = (7/20,\,69/100)$ via two explicit negative witnesses.
This note explains the underlying mechanism in terms of the $2\times2$
Gram matrix in the $\{P_0, P_2\}$ Legendre subspace, and corrects an
error in an earlier draft that attributed the failure to edge-mass
asymptotics.

\section{The 2x2 spectral mechanism}

In the Suzuki framework~\cite{WFP2026}, the Legendre basis $\{P_n\}$ is
unnormalised with Gram matrix $G_{ij} = \tfrac{2}{2i+1}\delta_{ij}$.
The kinetic term acts as $(T_N)_{ij} = H_{n_j}G_{ij}$ where
$H_n = \sum_{k=1}^n k^{-1}$ is the $n$-th harmonic number.
The Gram matrix entries of $\tilde{q}_L^\theta$ on $\{P_0, P_2\}$ are:
\begin{align*}
  E_{mn}^\theta
  &= H_{n_m}G_{mm}\delta_{mn}
     + \theta\,\ip{VP_n}{P_m}
     + \ip{K_LP_n}{P_m}
     - c_L\,G_{mm}\delta_{mn}.
\end{align*}

\begin{theorem}[Spectral mechanism for Path A failure]
\label{thm:mechanism}
At $L=7/20$, the $2\times2$ matrix $E^\theta$ has a strictly negative
eigenvalue for all $\theta\in[0, \theta_{\max}]$ where
$\theta_{\max} \approx 0.997$.  The negative direction is
$v^* = E_{22}P_0 - E_{02}P_2 \approx P_0 - P_2$.

The negativity is \emph{not} caused by the off-diagonal entry
$E_{02}^2 > E_{00}E_{22}$, but by $c_L > 0$ reducing the diagonal
entries below the level needed to dominate the off-diagonal.
Without $c_L$ (i.e., $c_L = 0$), the matrix is positive definite:
$\det E^\theta\big|_{c_L=0} \approx +1.48 > 0$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Direct computation from Remark~\ref{rem:values}:
$\det E^\theta = E_{00}E_{22} - (E_{02})^2 \approx 0.163\times0.222 - 0.218^2
\approx 0.036 - 0.047 = -0.011 < 0$.
Setting $c_L = 0$ removes the $-2.730$ from $E_{00}$ and $-0.546$ from
$E_{22}$, giving $E_{00}|_{c_L=0} \approx 2.893$ and
$E_{22}|_{c_L=0} \approx 0.768$, hence
$\det|_{c_L=0} \approx 2.893\times0.768 - 0.218^2 \approx 2.222 - 0.047 > 0$.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Why the Schur complement machine is necessary]
Because $\{P_0, P_2\}$ is positive definite when $c_L = 0$, a
redistribution-only argument ($\tilde{q}^\theta$ without the $c_L$
shift) cannot detect the obstruction.
The full Split-residual Schur criterion of~\cite{WFP2026} Theorem~5
is necessary precisely because it must overcome the global negative
shift $c_L I$ using the positive kinetic energy of high-order modes
$H_n \gg c_L$ for large $n$.
\end{corollary}

\section{Failure of the edge-mass asymptotic}

An earlier draft of this paper (now withdrawn) attempted to characterise
the Path A obstruction via the formula
$\theta_0(L) = 1 - \ctwo/\kappae(L)$, claiming this equals the zero of
the off-diagonal entry $E_{02}^\theta(L)$.  This was incorrect.

\begin{remark}[Lemma L3 fails in the physical window]
\label{rem:lemmaL3}
The companion paper's Lemma~L3 states
$\ip{K_LP_0}{P_2} \sim \kappae(L)^{-1}\ctwo$ as $\varepsilon\to0^+$,
where $\varepsilon = 2 - \log2/L$.
At $L = 7/20$, $\varepsilon \approx 0.020$ and the asymptotics give
$\ctwo/\kappae \approx 0.303$, while the certified value is
$\ip{K_LP_0}{P_2} \approx 0.00712$.
The $O(1)$ remainder ($\approx -0.296$) is the entire leading term;
the asymptotic provides no useful bound in the first-prime window.

As a consequence, the actual zero of $E_{02}^\theta$ satisfies:
\[
  \theta_{\mathrm{zero}} = \frac{c_2\gamma_{02} - \ip{K_LP_0}{P_2}}
                                  {\ip{VP_0}{P_2}}
  \approx \frac{0.0186 - 0.00712}{0.3333}
  \approx 0.034,
\]
far from $\theta_0 \approx 0.697$.
The formula $1-\ctwo/\kappae$ has no algebraic relationship to the
spectrum of $\tilde{q}^\theta$.
\end{remark}

\section{Summary}

\begin{enumerate}
\item Path A fails because the Weil constant $c_L \approx 1.365$
      globally shifts the diagonal entries of the $2\times2$ Gram matrix
      below the level needed for positive definiteness.
      Without $c_L$, the $\{P_0,P_2\}$ subspace is positive definite.

\item Lemma~L3's asymptotic $\mu_K^{(02)} \sim \ctwo/\kappae$
      fails by a factor of $40$ at $L=7/20$; any argument depending
      on it is invalid in the first-prime window.

\item The formula $\theta_0 = 1 - \ctwo/\kappae$ is not the zero of
      $E_{02}^\theta$.  Theorem~6 of~\cite{WFP2026} provides the
      correct (certified) falsification of Path A without requiring
      any asymptotic expansion.
\end{enumerate}
